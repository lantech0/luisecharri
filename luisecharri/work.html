How I Approach an Audit

I run full-site technical, on-page, and competitive audits using Ahrefs, Screaming Frog, Siteliner, BuzzSumo, Link Research Tools, Searchmetrics, Google Analytics, PageSpeed Insights, and Google Trends. Findings get compiled into audit reports and delivered via Dropbox or Google Drive, with the workload managed in Asana and coordinated through Slack. On the on-page side, that work includes auditing and resolving duplicate, missing, or truncated title tags and meta descriptions, and fixing missing or duplicate H1 tags. When a project calls for outreach research, I identify decision-makers using LinkedIn Sales Navigator, validate their contact data with Skrapp and Excel, and deliver upload-ready lead lists.
