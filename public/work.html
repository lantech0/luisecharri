The Same Four Steps, Every Time

Here's the exact process I run on every audit—the same one behind seven-plus years of client work—plus a real example of it in action and the framework behind it.

How I Approach an Audit

  1. Full-site technical & competitive review Crawl, indexation, and competitive gaps, using Ahrefs, Screaming Frog, Siteliner, Searchmetrics, Link Research Tools, PageSpeed Insights, Google Analytics, Google Trends & BuzzSumo.
  2. On-page fixes Duplicate, missing, or truncated title tags and meta descriptions, plus missing or duplicate H1 tags.
  3. Reporting Findings written up as an actionable report, delivered through Dropbox or Google Drive, deadlines tracked in Asana, day-to-day coordination over Slack.
  4. Decision-maker research (when a project calls for outreach) Identifying the right contacts through LinkedIn Sales Navigator, validating their details with Skrapp and Excel, and packaging everything into a clean, upload-ready list.
