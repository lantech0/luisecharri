How I Approach an Audit

Each audit starts with a full-site technical and competitive review, then moves into the on-page details—duplicate, missing, or truncated title tags and meta descriptions, plus missing or duplicate H1 tags—using tools like Ahrefs, Screaming Frog, and PageSpeed Insights along the way. Findings get written up as a report and delivered through Dropbox or Google Drive, with deadlines tracked in Asana and day-to-day coordination over Slack. When a project also calls for outreach, decision-maker research runs alongside it: identifying the right contacts through LinkedIn Sales Navigator, validating their details with Skrapp and Excel, and packaging everything into a list that's ready to upload.
