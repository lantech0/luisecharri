What I Do

SEO Audits, Site Builds, and Lead Research

Three areas make up the core of my work: technical and on-page SEO audits, decision-maker research for outreach, and WordPress site builds. Each one draws on tools and processes from across my seven-plus years in SEO and research roles, from Ahrefs and Screaming Frog to LinkedIn Sales Navigator and Skrapp.

Core Skill

Technical & On-Page SEO Audits

I run full-site technical, on-page, and competitive audits using Ahrefs, Screaming Frog, Siteliner, Searchmetrics, Link Research Tools, and PageSpeed Insights—covering site structure, crawl issues, and competitive gaps. On the on-page side, I audit and resolve duplicate, missing, or truncated title tags and meta descriptions, and fix missing or duplicate H1 tags across every page that needs it.

Core Skill

Lead & Decision-Maker Research

I identify decision-makers using LinkedIn Sales Navigator, then validate their contact data with Skrapp and Excel before building out the list. The result is a clean, upload-ready lead list, organized for outreach and lead-generation campaigns.

Core Skill

WordPress Site Builds

I build and launch full WordPress sites from scratch, from initial setup and structure through to a live, published site. For local businesses, that also includes building and optimizing local citations and Google Business Profile listings to improve visibility in local search and the map pack.
